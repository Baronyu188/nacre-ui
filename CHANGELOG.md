# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-08-27

- Reworked the shared light and dark glass material with path-aligned rim, glow, and refraction controls.
- Added the standalone Glass Lab for tuning and comparing material settings.
- Added reusable `Stepper`, `Toast`, `AlertDialog`, `CommandPalette`, `MenuButton`, and `ContextMenu` components.
- Added left, right, top, and bottom placements to drawers and `AdaptiveSheet`.
- Improved tabs, menus, navigation, notifications, form controls, and pointer interactions.
- Expanded public exports, package verification, documentation, gallery coverage, and the bundled Nacre Agent Skill.
